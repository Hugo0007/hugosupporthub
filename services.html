Services

From AI-curious to AI-run.

One arc, three moves. Start anywhere — most engagements run all three in order, because understanding without a build evaporates, and a build without teaching gets abandoned.

01 — Translate 02 — Build 03 — Teach
01 / TRANSLATE

Make it make sense

Before anything gets built, you need a clear map: what AI actually does, what it costs, and where it genuinely fits your business — minus the hype and the jargon.

  • a

    AI clarity sessions

    Working sessions that walk your actual workflow and mark, in plain language, where AI helps, where it doesn't, and why.

  • b

    Tool evaluations

    Straight answers on the tools you're considering — what they do well, what the pricing hides, and whether a lighter option exists.

  • c

    Opportunity mapping

    A ranked list of the automations worth building in your business, ordered by hours saved against effort to ship.

You leave with → a plain-language AI roadmap for your business — the what, the why, and the order to build in.
02 / BUILD

Ship the system

Working automations, wired into the tools you already use. No-code where possible, AI where it earns its place — and everything documented so nothing is a black box.

  • a

    Workflow automation

    Response handling, follow-up sequences, CRM updates and reporting built end-to-end with Make, Zapier or n8n.

  • b

    AI integrations

    Claude and other AI APIs wired into your workflows — drafting, sorting, scoring and summarizing the work your team repeats every day.

  • c

    Operations systems

    Airtable-backed systems that give a growing business one source of truth — pipeline, delivery, and the numbers that matter.

You leave with → live, documented systems in your own accounts. Past builds have saved clients 10–25+ hours weekly.
03 / TEACH

Hand you the controls

A system nobody understands gets abandoned in a quarter. Every engagement ends with your team able to run, adjust and extend what was built — inside their own context.

  • a

    Operator onboarding

    Hands-on sessions in your tools, your data, your workflows — not generic tutorials. Your team learns by running the real thing.

  • b

    Founder AI coaching

    One-on-one for founders who want to personally understand and drive the AI side of their business, including Claude Code onboarding.

  • c

    Playbooks & documentation

    Every build ships with plain-language documentation: what runs when, what to check, and how to change it safely.

You leave with → a team that owns the system, and documentation that outlives the engagement.

The tools already exist.

The edge is understanding them.
