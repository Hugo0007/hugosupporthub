Privacy policy

Last updated: July 2026

What we collect

This site collects personal information only when you choose to send it — through the Work With Us form (name, email, business context, and your message) or by booking a call through Calendly (handled under Calendly's own privacy policy).

We do not run advertising trackers and do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

How it's used

  • Form submissions are emailed to the founder's inbox and used solely to respond to your enquiry.
  • If we work together, your details are used to deliver the engagement.
  • You will not be added to a mailing list without explicitly signing up for one.

Where it lives

This site is hosted on Vercel. Form submissions pass through Vercel's serverless functions and are delivered by Resend (an email service) to our inbox. Both providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

Your choices

Want your information corrected or deleted? Email ugochukwuokoronkwo24@gmail.com and it will be handled promptly.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.
